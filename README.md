# 🧠 Self-Companion — A Personal Recovery & Organization App

**Self-Companion** is a modular desktop app designed to help people like me — neurodivergent, trauma survivors, people rebuilding their lives — manage their thoughts, emotions, tasks, and personal growth in one private, focused space.

I created this project to help myself survive, reflect, and regain control. I’m sharing it publicly because I know I’m not the only one who needs something like this.

---

## 💡 What It Does (Eventually)

Self-Companion is split into a few sections:

- **Therapy Corner** – Private journaling and AI chat, all saved locally
- **Organization Zone** – Notes, spider web-style visual maps, task calendar with priorities
- **Rediscovery Zone** – Mood tracking, play logs, self-reflection prompts

Everything is **local-first**, with a strong emphasis on **privacy, modularity, and personal customization**.
